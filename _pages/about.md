## About

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Junteng is pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at HKUST. Junteng graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Current research interests are LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.
